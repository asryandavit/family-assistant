# Family Coach — Interview (Davit)

**How to use this:** type your answer after the bold **A** line under each question. Full sentences, fragments, whatever. For multiple-choice questions just type the number.

Skip anything you'd rather not answer. Only the few marked **[required]** are genuinely needed — those are the numbers the calculators cannot run without.

Take it in sittings; Google saves as you go. Tell the bot when you're done and it reads this document.

Be honest rather than aspirational, especially about time. A plan built on your best week is a plan you'll follow for one week.
