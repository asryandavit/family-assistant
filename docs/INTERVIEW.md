# INTERVIEW.md — question catalogue, v1

For review before anything is built. See `docs/COACH_SPEC.md` §14 and decision 061.

---

## Delivery

Two Google Forms, one per adult, generated by an Apps Script kept in this repo. Davit
runs the script from his account, wife runs it from hers, so each owns their own
responses (decision 052). The Telegram bot sends the link and reads the response Sheet
when told the form is complete.

**Not in the Form — asked privately in Telegram instead:** the postpartum symptom screen
and medical clearance. Decision 036 says that tier is never mirrored to any Sheet, and a
Form response Sheet is a Sheet in Drive.

Legend: **[calc]** feeds a calculator directly · **[gate]** changes what the system is
allowed to do · **[model]** free text read by the model at generation time, never parsed
by code.

## Section 1 — Basics (10)

These drive BMR, TDEE, calorie and protein targets. Everything else is refinement;
without these the system cannot function.

1. **Your name** — short text.
2. **Sex** — Male / Female. **[calc]** Mifflin–St Jeor uses different constants.
3. **Date of birth** — date. **[calc]**
4. **Height in cm** — number, 120–220. **[calc]**
5. **Current weight in kg** — number, 30–250, decimals allowed. **[calc]**
6. **How active is your day, honestly?** **[calc]** Match a normal week, not a good week.
   - Mostly sitting, little deliberate movement (×1.2)
   - Light activity or exercise 1–3 days a week (×1.375)
   - Moderate activity or exercise 3–5 days a week (×1.55)
   - Hard activity or exercise 6–7 days a week (×1.725)
7. **Track your waist as well as weight?** — Yes / No. **[gate]** Shown with the reason:
   when you build muscle while losing fat, the scale can sit still for weeks while your
   waist keeps shrinking, and without a waist measurement the system can misread that as
   a stall. Optional, changeable later.
8. **Are you currently breastfeeding?** — Yes / No / Not applicable. **[gate]**
9. **Any medical condition, medication or diagnosis affecting diet or exercise?** — long
   text, optional. **[model]** This bot is not a medical provider.
10. **What does success look like to you in six months?** — long text. **[model]**

## Section 2 — Training history (8)

11. **What structured training have you done before?** — checkboxes: gym / weights ·
    home workouts · running or cycling · yoga or pilates · team sport · martial arts ·
    swimming · nothing structured · other.
12. **When were you last training consistently, and for how long?** — short text.
13. **Why did it stop?** — long text. **[model]** *The most useful answer in this form.*
    Time, boredom, injury, a baby, results plateaued, it got dark earlier — whatever it
    actually was. Be blunt; the plan is built around avoiding a repeat.
14. **Longest you've ever kept a routine going?** — under a month / 1–3 months /
    3–6 months / 6–12 months / over a year.
15. **Injury history** — long text, optional. **[model]** Past injuries, surgeries, joints
    that complain — not just current problems.
16. **Anything hurting right now?** — long text, optional. **[gate]**
17. **Have you trained at home before? What worked and what didn't?** — long text.
    **[model]**
18. **How confident do you feel doing an exercise correctly without someone watching?** —
    1–5. Drives how much coaching detail appears on each session card.

## Section 3 — Movement self-screen (8)

Sets a conservative starting level only. Preamble shown to both: this is a rough
self-assessment, not a test. Under-report rather than over-report — the first two weeks
of real sessions do the actual calibration, and starting too easy costs one week while
starting too hard costs an injury.

19. **Squat** — heels flat, thighs roughly parallel? Yes comfortably · yes but a struggle ·
    only partway · heels lift or it hurts.
20. **Push-ups — how many in a row with good form?** — number, 0+.
21. **Which push-up can you currently do?** — full on toes · on knees · hands on a chair ·
    hands on a wall.
22. **Plank — seconds in a good position** — number.
23. **Single-leg balance — seconds, eyes open** — number.
24. **Straight legs, how far toward your toes?** — palms flat · fingertips to floor ·
    ankles · mid-shin · knees.
25. **Any position or movement that causes pain?** — long text, optional. **[gate]** An
    answer here locks the relevant movements until resolved.
26. **How does your body feel the day after activity?** — fine · mildly sore · very sore
    for days · unpredictable.

## Section 4 — Life, energy and time (12)

Adherence beats optimality. This section decides whether the plan is possible at all.

27. **Typical hours of sleep on a weeknight** — number, 0–12.
28. **Sleep quality** — 1 (broken and unrefreshing) to 5 (solid).
29. **How often does your son wake you at night?** — most nights · a few times a week ·
    rarely · not any more.
30. **Current stress level** — 1 (calm) to 5 (constantly stretched).
31. **What already helps with stress?** — long text, optional. **[model]** The plan should
    extend what works, not replace it.
32. **When can you realistically train?** — checkboxes: before he wakes · morning nap ·
    afternoon nap · lunchtime · after he goes to bed · weekend mornings · weekend
    afternoons · it varies too much to say.
33. **Realistically, how many days a week?** — 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6. A normal week, not your
    best week.
34. **How long can a session realistically be?** — 15 / 20 / 30 / 45 minutes.
35. **What movement do you actually enjoy?** — checkboxes: strength · fast and sweaty ·
    yoga flows · stretching and mobility · breathwork and wind-down · walking · dancing ·
    none of it, I just want the result · other.
36. **What do you hate, or simply won't do?** — long text. **[model]** Burpees, jumping,
    anything on your knees, early mornings — say so and it won't appear.
37. **Yoga and mobility experience** — never tried · tried a few times · did it regularly ·
    currently do it.
38. **What would you want from yoga or mobility work?** — checkboxes: flexibility · stress
    relief · better sleep · injury prevention · not interested.

## Section 5 — Kitchen and food (19)

Meals arrive a few weeks after training. This is answered now so that when it does
arrive, it starts from your real kitchen rather than a generic one.

39. **Which days do you usually cook?** — checkboxes Mon–Sun. **[gate]** Each day's meal
    is validated against that day's cook's time limit.
40. **Cooking skill** — 1 (basics only) to 5 (confident with anything).
41. **How do you feel about following a new recipe?** — happily · fine if it's simple ·
    prefer what I know.
42. **On a weekday, how many minutes of ACTIVE cooking are you willing to do?** —
    10 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 45. **[gate]** *Active* = hands-on: chopping, stirring, standing
    at the hob. Oven time doesn't count. Hard limit — the plan may not exceed it.
43. **And on a weekend day?** — 15 / 20 / 30 / 45 / 60+. **[gate]**
44. **What's in your kitchen?** — checkboxes: oven · 4 hob burners · fewer than 4 ·
    slow cooker · pressure cooker · air fryer · microwave · blender · food processor ·
    hand blender.
45. **Do you have kitchen scales?** — Yes / No. **[gate]** Portions are given in grams. If
    no, the plan switches to cups, spoons and household measures — less precise but
    workable.
46. **Freezer space: roughly how many meal-sized containers?** — none · 2–4 · 5–10 ·
    10–20 · more than 20. **[gate]** Caps how much batch cooking gets scheduled.
47. **Which dishes do you already cook regularly?** — long text, **names only**.
    **[model]** 10–15 is ideal. The plan starts from these and adjusts portions and
    protein before introducing anything new. Ingredients and quantities come later, one
    dish at a time.
48. **Which of those does your family actually love?** — long text. **[model]**
49. **Foods you will not eat** — long text. **[model]**
50. **Allergies or intolerances, anyone in the family** — long text, optional. **[gate]**
51. **How many days in a row will you happily eat the same dish?** — 1 · 2 · 3 · more.
52. **What does breakfast look like at the moment?** — long text. **[model]** Usually the
    most fixed meal, and the easiest to leave alone.
53. **How often do you eat out or order in?** — rarely · a few times a month · weekly ·
    several times a week.
54. **Where do you shop, and how often?** — long text. **[model]** SAS, Yerevan City,
    Carrefour, Evrika, GUM market, local shops — and one big weekly trip versus frequent
    small ones.
55. **What does your son eat on a typical day?** — long text. **[model]** Roughly what and
    when. No calorie counting for him, ever — this is so meals can be adapted for him
    rather than cooked separately.
56. **Anything he refuses, or textures that don't work?** — long text. **[model]**
57. **How many new dishes a week are you willing to try?** — 0 · 1 · 2. Novel dishes every
    week is how a plan gets abandoned in week three.

## Asked in Telegram, not in the Form

**Postpartum screen** (wife only, private): diastasis or doming when sitting up · leaking
when coughing, sneezing or jumping · heaviness or pressure in the pelvis · whether a
pelvic-floor physiotherapist has been seen · whether a doctor has cleared impact exercise.

Until these are answered and clear, training stays at tier 0 (breathing, deep-core
activation, glute bridge, bird-dog) and impact movements stay locked. Postpartum is not
the same as breastfeeding — at 19 months these considerations have not expired.

## Notes for review

- **57 questions**, five sections, roughly 15–20 minutes. Cut anything not worth its answer.
- **Q13** ("why did it stop") is the highest-value item here — keep it even under a hard trim.
- **Q45** (scales) changes how every portion in the system is expressed.
- **Q42/Q43** become hard constraints in code. Both adults should answer pessimistically,
  not aspirationally.
- Questions marked **[calc]** cannot be dropped without breaking the calculators.
