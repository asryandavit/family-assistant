# Family Coach — Interview

**What this is:** Davit is building a small assistant that will send a short daily training plan and a weekly meal plan for the family. Before it suggests anything, it needs to know how you actually live — not how a fitness app assumes you live.

**How to use this:** type your answer after the bold **A** line under each question. Full sentences, fragments, whatever comes out. For multiple-choice questions just type the number or the words. Google saves as you go, so take it in as many sittings as you like.

Skip anything you'd rather not answer. Only the few marked **[required]** are genuinely needed, and those are just the numbers the calculations can't run without.

**Please be honest rather than optimistic**, especially about time and energy. A plan built around your best week is a plan that works for one week and then makes you feel like you failed. There are no wrong answers here and nothing is being judged.

A few private questions about your body and recovery aren't in this document — those come separately and privately through the bot in Telegram, and stay only there.

**Q7.** Would you like to track your waist measurement as well as your weight? — Yes / No

Completely optional, and you can change your mind at any time. The reason it helps: when you build a little muscle while losing fat, the scale can sit still for weeks even though your waist is shrinking. Without a waist measurement the system can misread that as "nothing is working" and reduce your food when it shouldn't.

**Please under-report rather than over-report.** Starting too easy costs a week. Starting too hard costs an injury.

## Section 5 — Kitchen and food

Meal planning arrives a few weeks after the training side starts. These are answered now so that when it does, it starts from your real kitchen rather than a generic one.

The plan is always **one meal for the whole family** — the same dish for everyone, with different portion sizes, and adjustments for your son built in. Never separate cooking.
