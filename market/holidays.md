# Armenian public holidays (non-working days) — used for working-day arrival dates

Reviewed each January. One date per line as `- YYYY-MM-DD · name`; only lines in that shape
are read (market/workdays.py). Working days = Monday–Friday that are not listed here.
A holiday on a weekend is not moved (no substitute day is listed unless the government
decides one; such decisions are added here by hand with their decree).

Sources (checked 2026-10-29): qppstudio.net Armenia 2026 and 2027 pages; 4 May 2026 made a
non-working day by Government decision 109-N (working Saturday 18 April 2026 instead).
