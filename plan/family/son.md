# Son — plan (~22 months, turning 2 in December 2026)

Not a "training" plan and never a diet. The goal is lots of play, good sleep, and the
same family food made safe for him.

## Movement — WHO guidance for ages 1–2

| Guideline | Target |
|---|---|
| Active play | **≥ 180 minutes a day**, any intensity, spread through the day |
| Restrained (pram, high chair, car seat, carrier) | **≤ 1 hour at a time** |
| Screens | none is best; from age 2 **≤ 1 hour a day**, less is better |
| Sleep | **11–14 hours** including the nap |

From age 3 the target adds ≥ 60 min of energetic play within the 180 minutes.

## Daily play menu (mix and match)

| When | Ideas |
|---|---|
| Morning, outdoors (45–60 min) | Playground: climbing, slide, swings; walking on uneven ground; chasing bubbles; kicking and throwing a ball; pushing his own stroller |
| Indoors, any time | Cushion obstacle course; crawling through a "tunnel" of chairs; dancing to music; animal walks (bear crawl, frog hops, crab); rolling a ball back and forth; stacking and carrying toys |
| **18:15 family movement time** | Parents' warm-up together: march, dance, shoulder rolls; he copies the KID-flagged moves (`ex-sit-stand`, `ex-birddog`, `cd-march`, `cd-dance`, `mb-cat-cow`) — as a game, never as reps |
| Evening, calm | Bath, books, slow songs — no screens in the last hour before bed |

Safety during parents' workouts: keep him out from under planks and push-ups, and
never lift him as "weight" in a way that strains your back.

## Food — same dishes, toddler-safe

He eats **what the family eats**, with these changes:

- **Full-fat dairy** — whole milk, matsun, katnashor. The adults' low-fat and lactose
  choices do **not** apply to him.
- **No added salt or sugar** — take his portion out before seasoning; no honey-sweetened
  extras beyond a little.
- **Soft textures and safe cuts:** grapes and cherry tomatoes quartered; no whole nuts
  (ground or as nut butter only); no popcorn; raw carrot/apple grated or cooked soft;
  meat in small soft pieces; check fish for bones.
- **Iron and protein daily:** eggs, lentils, chicken, beef/meat occasionally, fish.
  Citrus and other fruit are fine for him — the citrus limit is Davit's only.
- **Water** as the main drink; no juice or sweet drinks.
- Normal portions — let him decide how much (no "finish your plate").

## Small habits that help the whole family

- Eat together at the table — he learns by copying the vegetables on your plates.
- Same wake/nap/bedtime anchors every day ([../daily-rhythm.md](../daily-rhythm.md)) —
  a predictable toddler is what makes the parents' plan possible.
