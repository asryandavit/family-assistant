## The design idea: fit around the toddler, don't fight him

- Sessions are **short (10–25 min)** and live in the **nap window** (≈13:45–15:30) or
  in family movement time at 18:15.
- **Walking with the son counts** — the daily playground walk is already half the
  cardio.
- The son can **join** the easy moves (marked KID in the library) — copying Mum is good
  play for him and keeps him busy. Not under you during planks or push-ups.
- Cooking time is cut by batch cooking twice a week (see [../meals/](../meals/)), so
  training doesn't compete with dinner.

## Postpartum gate — read first

Full rules: [../training/progression.md](../training/progression.md#postpartum-gate).
Short version: check for **leaking, pelvic heaviness/pressure, pelvic or low-back pain,
or belly doming**. Any of these → stay on **Foundations**, skip PP/IMPACT moves, see a
**pelvic-floor physiotherapist**. Progress is by symptoms, not by the calendar. This is
private — it doesn't need to be written anywhere.

## Training program — 12 weeks

Exercises by id from [../training/exercises.md](../training/exercises.md).

### Weeks 1–2 · Foundations — daily 8–12 min (everyone starts here)

| # | id | dose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `br-360` | 8 breaths |
| 2 | `br-ta` (deep core + pelvic floor on the exhale) | 10 reps |
| 3 | `ex-bridge` | 10–12 |
| 4 | `ex-birddog` | 6 / side |
| 5 | `ex-deadbug-heel` | 6 / side |
| 6 | `ex-pushup-wall` | 8–10 |
| 7 | `ex-sit-stand` | 10 |

Plus: **daily walk 20–30 min** with the son (`cd-walk`), 5 min mobility in the evening
(`mb-cat-cow`, `mb-child`, `mb-hip-flexor`, `mb-doorway`).

### Weeks 3–8 · Build — 3 × 15–20 min (Mon / Wed / Fri, nap time)

Only if the self-check is clear; otherwise repeat Foundations and see the physio.
2 rounds → 3 rounds by week 6, RPE 5–6.

**Session 1**

| # | id | reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ex-chair-squat` | 10–12 |
| 2 | `ex-pushup-counter` | 8–10 |
| 3 | `ex-bridge` → `ex-bridge-march` (PP: only when clear) | 12 / 6 per side |
| 4 | `ex-birddog-hold` | 6 / side |
| 5 | `ex-calf` | 15 |

**Session 2**

| # | id | reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ex-lunge-assist` | 6–8 / side |
| 2 | `ex-plank-incline` | 20–30 s |
| 3 | `ex-deadbug-heel` | 8 / side |
| 4 | `ex-side-plank-knee` (PP) | 15 s / side |
| 5 | `cd-step-jack` | 3 × 40 s |

Daily walks **30–45 min**, brisk part of the time (`cd-walk-brisk`) — the stroller helps.

### Weeks 9–12 · Strengthen — 3 strength × 25 min + 1–2 cardio

Each exercise moves up its own ladder by the level-up rule. Cardio (Tue / Sat):
`cd-circuit-low` 3 rounds or a brisk 30–40 min walk. **IMPACT moves only after several
symptom-free weeks at level 2, ideally after a physio check.**

## Daily mobility and calm (5–10 min)

Toddler life is the stress side of the plan, and recovery matters as much as training:
`br-360` or `br-wind` before sleep, `mb-cat-cow`, `mb-open-book`, `mb-child`.

## Food specifics

Shared family meals: [../meals/](../meals/). Karine's own notes:

- Same plate rule as Davit: ½ vegetables, ¼ protein, ¼ grains/legumes; one small piece
  of bread per meal.
- **Eat your own meals sitting down** — not the toddler's leftovers standing up (a
  classic hidden source of calories).
- Snack 15:30 with coffee #2: fruit or matsun, not biscuits.
- No fasting needed — regular meals make the day easier with a toddler.
