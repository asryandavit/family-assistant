# Davit — personal plan

## Profile

- Software developer, sits most of the day. Beginner, currently sedentary.
- **Goals:** lose weight (primary), lower LDL cholesterol, get the body moving, less
  neck/back stiffness.
- **Health:** mild gastritis · high cholesterol · overweight · possible lactose issue
  from **fresh milk only** · avoid **citrus** (lemon, orange, grapefruit) — "not much"
  rather than never.
- **Likes:** coffee twice a day (relax time) · learning to cook from Scoolinary:
  pizza, croissants, chocolate, beer brewing.

## What actually moves the needle (honest version)

1. **Food does most of the weight loss.** Exercise adds only a little on top of
   diet — but it protects muscle, improves blood pressure, sugar and mood, and makes the
   loss stick. Do both; expect the scale to follow the kitchen.
2. **For LDL, food matters most too:** oats and legumes (soluble fibre), fish, olive
   oil instead of butter, little red/processed meat. Exercise lowers LDL only modestly.
3. **For a developer, breaking up sitting is its own treatment** — separate from the
   workout. Someone who trains 30 minutes but sits 10 hours straight is still sitting 10 hours.

## 1. Desk protocol (every workday, from day 1)

| Habit | How |
|---|---|
| **Break every 30 min** | Stand up for 2–5 min. Best: a short walk (corridor, balcony, kitchen). Minimum: `mb-desk-reset`. Use a timer or tie it to "after every commit / every meeting". |
| **Eyes 20-20-20** | Every 20 min look at something ~6 m away for 20 s. Low effort, likely helps; evidence is thin. |
| **Posture reset** | `mb-chin-tuck` + `mb-shoulder-roll` at each break. |
| **Calls standing / walking** | Any call without screen-share. |
| **Exercise snacks** | 2–3 times a day, 1–2 min brisk: climb 2–4 floors of stairs, or 20 fast sit-to-stands. |
| **Water bottle on the desk** | Refill = an extra break. |
| **After lunch** | 10–15 min walk. Gentle is fine on a full stomach and blunts the sugar rise. |

**Steps target:** measure your normal week first (phone counts steps). Then add about
**+1,000 steps/day each week** while it feels fine, towards **7,000–8,000/day** — the
range where the health benefit is strong. More is fine, not required.

## 2. Training program — 12 weeks

All exercises are from [../training/exercises.md](../training/exercises.md) by id.
Effort and level-up rules: [../training/progression.md](../training/progression.md).
**Never within 2–3 h after lunch** — default slot **18:15**, ~5 h after lunch.

Every session: **warm-up 3 min** (`cd-march` 1 min, `mb-shoulder-roll`, `mb-cat-cow`, 5 ×
`ex-sit-stand`) and **cool-down 3 min** (`mb-hip-flexor`, `mb-hamstring`, `mb-doorway`).

### Weeks 1–4 · Foundations — 3 × 15–20 min (Mon / Wed / Fri)

Circuit: do the list in order, rest 30–60 s between exercises.
Week 1–2: **2 rounds** · Week 3–4: **3 rounds** · RPE 5–6.

**Strength A**

| # | id | reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ex-sit-stand` → `ex-chair-squat` | 10–12 |
| 2 | `ex-pushup-wall` → `ex-pushup-counter` | 8–12 |
| 3 | `ex-bridge` | 12–15 |
| 4 | `ex-birddog` | 6 / side |
| 5 | `ex-prone-ytw` (desk antidote) | 1 round of Y-T-W |

**Strength B**

| # | id | reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ex-lunge-assist` | 6–8 / side |
| 2 | `ex-pushup-counter` | 8–12 |
| 3 | `ex-deadbug-heel` | 6–8 / side |
| 4 | `ex-calf` | 15 |
| 5 | `ex-side-plank-knee` | 15–20 s / side |

Other days: walk (+ the daily step target). Saturday: `cd-stairs` a few floors, easy.

### Weeks 5–8 · Build — 3 strength × 25 min + 2 cardio × 15–20 min

Strength A/B stay the same shape; each exercise moves up its own ladder when the
level-up rule says so (e.g. `ex-chair-squat` → `ex-squat`, `ex-pushup-counter` →
`ex-pushup-sofa`, `ex-plank-incline` → `ex-plank-knee`). 3 rounds, RPE 6–7.

**Cardio (Tue / Sat):** `cd-stairs` (walk up 3–4 floors, down easy, repeat 15 min) or
`cd-circuit-low` 3 rounds. Talk-test moderate.

### Weeks 9–12 · Strengthen — 4 sessions × 30 min

2 × strength (3–4 rounds, RPE 7) + 2 × cardio (`cd-stairs-int` or `cd-circuit-low`,
20–30 min). Optional: `cd-circuit-impact` if knees feel fine.

Weekly total by week 12 ≈ **150 min of moderate activity + 2 strength days**, plus
walking — the adult guideline. For bigger weight loss the evidence points to more
(~250 min/week); walking is the easiest way to get there.

## 3. Daily mobility (5–10 min, every day)

`mb-cat-cow`, `mb-hip-flexor`, `mb-hamstring`, `mb-wrist`. Before sleep: `br-wind` 3–5 min.

## 4. Food specifics

Shared family meals: [../meals/](../meals/). Davit's own adjustments:

- **Milk:** lactose-free milk (or none) in oats/coffee. **Matsun, katnashor (tvorog) and
  aged cheese are fine in normal portions** — fermented and curd dairy carry much less
  lactose, and live-culture yogurt like matsun helps digest it.
- **No citrus dressings** — olive oil + herbs, a little garlic, mild vinegar if tolerated.
- **Gastritis:** regular meals, no long fasting (no intermittent fasting — not needed for
  weight loss and fasting raises stomach acid), not too spicy, nothing deep-fried, light
  dinner by 19:00.
- **Coffee ×2:** keep it. If the stomach complains, have it after food, not first thing
  on an empty stomach; last cup by ~16:00 for sleep. Black or with lactose-free milk
  keeps it near zero calories.
- **Portions (no counting):** plate = ½ vegetables, ¼ protein (palm-size), ¼ grains or
  legumes (fist-size). Bread/lavash: one small piece per meal.
- **Treats:** the Scoolinary project is the planned treat — enjoy a proper portion, share
  the rest. Beer brewing is a great hobby; drinking is best kept occasional (alcohol
  irritates the stomach and adds calories).
