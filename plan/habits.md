# Habits — how to make the plan stick

A plan only works if it survives a bad week. These techniques are the ones with
the best evidence. Realistic expectation: a new habit takes about **two months** to feel
automatic on average, with a huge spread (some people weeks, some much longer). Missing
a single day does not break it.

## 1. If–then plans (implementation intentions)

Write the exact *when + where + what*. Plans phrased as "if X, then I do Y" roughly
double follow-through compared to just intending.

- Davit: *"If it's 18:00 on Mon/Wed/Fri, then I close the laptop, change clothes and do
  Strength A/B in the living room."*
- Karine: *"If the son falls asleep after lunch on Mon/Wed/Fri, then I roll out the mat
  before anything else."*
- Both: *"If I finish lunch, then I walk 10 minutes."*

## 2. Stack on habits you already have

Attach the new thing to something that already happens every day:

| After… | …do |
|---|---|
| Coffee #1 | 5 min mobility |
| Every commit / meeting end (Davit) | Stand up, 2–5 min desk break |
| Closing the laptop | Change into training clothes |
| Son's bedtime | 10 min mobility + tomorrow's 2-minute check |
| Brushing teeth at night | Wind-down breathing |

## 3. Make it easy, make it obvious

- Mat stays rolled out in a corner; training clothes on a hook.
- Water bottle on the desk; fruit visible in the kitchen; no snacks at the desk.
- The week's plan printed or open on the fridge / phone.

## 4. Minimum version on bad days

Sick toddler, bad night, deadline: do the **minimum** — one round, or a 10-minute walk —
and tick it. Keeping the chain alive matters more than the dose.
**Rule: never miss twice in a row.**

## 5. Do it together

When one partner becomes more active, the other is far more likely to follow — the
strongest predictor in couples studies. Use it: shared calendar, family movement time at
18:15, Saturday weigh-in together, cheer each other's ticks.

## 6. Track lightly

Weekly, not hourly: weight + waist on Saturday, sessions ticked, average steps, one line
on how the week felt → [tracking.md](tracking.md). That's enough data to steer.

## 7. Sleep is part of the plan

Aim for **7+ hours**. People who sleep more eat noticeably less the next day. The 22:30
wind-down and the 16:00 coffee cut-off exist for this.

## 8. The Sunday 10-minute review (both, 21:00)

1. Look at the week's ticks and the Saturday numbers.
2. One thing that worked → keep. One thing that didn't → change one thing in the plan
   files (or ask Claude to).
3. Pick next week's Scoolinary slot and confirm the shopping list.
4. Any level-ups due? ([training/progression.md](training/progression.md))

## 9. States, not failures

Illness, travel, a toddler sleep regression are **pauses**, not failures. Write "paused"
in the log and restart from the same level. No punishment workouts, no guilt language,
80/20.
