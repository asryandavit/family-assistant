# Sources

Evidence behind the numbers in this plan (researched October 2026). Guidance, not
personal medical advice. "Weak" marks thin or observational evidence.

| Topic | Finding used in the plan | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Adult activity | 150–300 min/week moderate aerobic + strength ≥ 2 days | [WHO 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7719906/), [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html) |
| Progression | No evidence for the "10 % per week" rule → progress by tolerance | (secondary) [Outside](https://www.outsideonline.com/health/running/training-advice/running-101/myth-of-the-10-percent-rule/) |
| Exercise & lipids | Exercise lowers LDL ≈ 7 mg/dL (148 RCTs) — modest | [Sports Med 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39331324/) |
| Diet for LDL | Saturated fat 5–6 % of calories, DASH-style pattern | [AHA/ACC](https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/Journal-Scans/2014/09/04/17/26/2013-AHA-ACC-Guideline-on-Lifestyle-Management) |
| Weight loss dose | > 250 min/week for clinically significant loss; diet + exercise combined | [ACSM 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19127177/) |
| Diet vs exercise | Diet is the main driver; exercise adds ~1 kg but improves health regardless | [Cochrane](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003817_exercise-overweight-or-obesity) |
| Sitting breaks | 5-min light walk every 30 min best for blood sugar; all breaks lowered BP (weak: 11 people) | [Diaz 2023](https://scholars.mssm.edu/en/publications/breaking-up-prolonged-sitting-to-improve-cardiometabolic-risk-dos/) |
| Steps | Benefit strong by 7,000/day; plateau ~5,000–7,000 (observational) | [Lancet Public Health 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40713949/) |
| Exercise snacks | ~3 × 1–2 min vigorous/day linked to lower mortality (observational) | [Stamatakis 2022](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/publications/association-of-wearable-device-measured-vigorous-intermittent-lifestyle-physical-activity-with-mortality/) |
| 20-20-20 eyes | Low-risk; evidence thin and mixed (weak) | [Aston](https://research.aston.ac.uk/en/publications/the-effects-of-breaks-on-digital-eye-strain-dry-eye-and-binocular/) |
| Office neck pain | Neck/shoulder strengthening reduces it (27 RCTs) | [Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6093121/) |
| Postpartum | Red flags: leakage, heaviness, pelvic/low-back pain, doming → pelvic-health physio; progress by symptoms not dates | [Return-to-running guideline 2019](https://athleticsni.org/download/files/Returning_to_running_postnatal_guideline_for_medical_health_and_fitness_professionals_managing_this_population.05.pdf), [NHS Wales](https://abuhb.nhs.wales/files/physiotherapy/pelvic-health-physiotherapy-in-postnatal-care-returning-to-exercise-pdf/), [BJSM 2025](https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/59/8/562) |
| Toddlers 1–2 | ≥ 180 min/day activity; ≤ 1 h restrained; screens none at 1, ≤ 1 h at 2; sleep 11–14 h | [WHO 2019](https://www.who.int/news/item/24-04-2019-to-grow-up-healthy-children-need-to-sit-less-and-play-more) |
| Exercise after meals | Vigorous running 30 min after a meal increased reflux; light did not | [KoreaMed](https://koreamed.org/SearchBasic.php?RID=648254) |
| Late meals | Avoid eating 3–4 h before bed (NHS leaflets; one trust says ≥ 2 h) | [SW London](https://swlimo.southwestlondon.icb.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Self-Care-Information-for-Patients-Heartburn-and-Indigestion-V1.1.pdf), [UH Sussex](https://www.uhsussex.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Advice-on-management-of-gastro-oesophageal-reflux.pdf) |
| Fasting | Time-restricted eating no better than calorie restriction (12-month RCT) | [NEJM 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35443107/) |
| Fasting & stomach | Acid rises when fasting; mixed ulcer data, mostly Ramadan (weak) | [Review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10151003/) |
| Coffee | No link to ulcers in most studies; can trigger symptoms; empty vs with food not studied (weak) | [Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8778943) |
| Lactose | ~12 g (a cup of milk) often tolerated with meals; yogurt and hard cheese better tolerated; live cultures help | [NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/lactose-intolerance/eating-diet-nutrition), [EFSA](https://efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/1763) |
| Habits | If–then plans: medium-large effect; habit ~66 days median, range 18–254; one miss doesn't derail | [Gollwitzer & Sheeran](https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/2e749bfb-8533-437c-8203-7e788c910c5f), [Lally 2010](https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/how-form-habit), [Singh 2024](https://digital.library.adelaide.edu.au/items/fe6ba93e-1d6b-42b8-9ee7-a231a64ff006) |
| Partner effect | One partner's change strongly predicts the other's (observational, 50+) | [Jackson 2015](https://discovery-pp.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1460730/2/Jackson.2015_-_The_influence_of_partner%27s_behaviour...-1.pdf) |
| Beginner strength | Wall press-ups 3 × 5–10; sit-to-stand from a chair, 8–15 reps | [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-exercises/), [BHF](https://www.bhf.org.uk/informationsupport/heart-matters-magazine/activity/strength-exercises) |
| Sleep | Adults 7 h+; more sleep → ~270 kcal/day less eaten (RCT) | [AASM](https://aasm.org/seven-or-more-hours-of-sleep-per-night-a-health-necessity-for-adults/), [Tasali 2022](https://www.uchicagomedicine.org/forefront/research-and-discoveries-articles/2022/february/getting-more-sleep-reduces-caloric-intake) |

**Not evidence-based, used as common practice:** the exact progression ladders
(wall → incline → knee → full push-up, etc.), toddlers copying parents' workouts, and
the 2–3 h gap between a meal and a hard session.
