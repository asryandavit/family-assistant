# Progression and safety rules

Same rules for both adults. The son does not "progress" — he plays
(see [../family/son.md](../family/son.md)).

## How hard should it feel?

Use the **effort scale (RPE 1–10)** — how hard the last set felt:

| RPE | Feels like | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 3–4 | Easy, could chat freely | warm-ups, walking, mobility |
| 5–6 | Working, can talk in short sentences | weeks 1–4, most cardio |
| 7 | Hard, 3 more reps were possible | target for strength from week 5 |
| 8–10 | Very hard to maximal | **not used in this plan** |

**Talk test for cardio:** moderate = can talk, can't sing. Vigorous = only a few words.

## When to level up

There is no fixed weekly percentage — evidence does not support one. Progress by
tolerance:

> **Level up one exercise** when, for **two sessions in a row**, you finish every set at
> the top of the rep range, with good form, at **RPE ≤ 7**, and nothing hurts the next day
> beyond normal muscle soreness.

Change **one thing at a time**, in this order (same order the bot will use later):
**reps → slower tempo → bigger range → harder variation (next id in the library) → less rest**.

## When to step back

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Sharp or joint pain (not muscle burn) | Stop that exercise. Next session use the easier id. Pain twice → ask a doctor/physio. |
| Muscle soreness lasting > 3 days | Repeat the same level next week, don't add. |
| Sick, slept < 5 h, sick toddler at night | **Minimum version only** (one round, or a 10-min walk) — or skip. No guilt. |
| Missed a whole week | Restart one level lower for one week, then continue. |
| Stomach discomfort during exercise | Lower intensity, move the session further from meals. |

Nothing resets: travel, illness or a bad week pause the plan, they don't undo it.

## Gastritis timing (Davit, but sensible for everyone)

- **Gentle walk after a meal: fine and helpful** (10–15 min after lunch/dinner).
- **Strength or cardio sessions: 2–3 hours after a main meal.** Hard exercise soon after
  eating is what increases reflux; gentle exercise does not.
- Exercises flagged **MEAL** (planks, dead bugs, child's pose, lying face-down) never
  within ~2 hours of a main meal.
- Drink water during and after; not a big volume right before.

## Postpartum gate

Applies to **Karine**. ~22 months after birth, pelvic-floor and abdominal-wall recovery
still matters; it does not expire with time. Progress is by **symptoms and function, not
by dates**.

**Self-check before starting, and keep watching:**

- leaking urine (when coughing, laughing, lifting, jumping)
- heaviness, dragging or pressure in the pelvis
- pelvic or lower-back pain
- the belly "domes" or "cones" (a ridge down the middle) when sitting up or straining

**If any of these is present:** stay at **level 0** (foundations), do not do flagged
**PP** or **IMPACT** exercises, and book a **pelvic-floor physiotherapist** — this is
exactly what they treat, and pelvic-floor training has good evidence. The plan is not
medical care.

**If all clear:** progress normally through the levels. **IMPACT** moves (jumping) only
after several symptom-free weeks at level 2, and ideally after a physio check.
