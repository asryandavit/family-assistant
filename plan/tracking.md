# Tracking log

Simple weekly log — no app, no database. One row per week per person. Edit in place.

**Saturday weigh-in protocol:** morning, after the toilet, before eating, same scale,
light clothes. **Waist** (optional): at navel level, end of a normal breath out, tape
snug but not compressing. Look at the **4-week trend**, never one reading — weight jumps
±1 kg day to day from water and salt.
