# Tracking — light

No schedule, no pressure. The plan stays as it is unless you decide to change it.

## When you measure

- **Weight / waist** — whenever you feel like it. If you want to see a trend, jot the
  number in your own progress Sheet (Google Drive → Family Coach → **Progress — Davit**;
  Karine copies **Progress — template (make a copy)** into Karine's own Drive). Skipping
  weeks is fine.
- **Blood tests** — not needed for the plan. If one happens anyway, note the cholesterol
  numbers in your Sheet.
- **Tell Claude** a number or how things are going whenever you want an opinion or a
  change — "I'm down 3 kg, dinners feel too small", "LDL came back 3.9".

Health numbers stay in your own Sheet, not in this repo (decision 079). The postpartum
self-check is never written anywhere — it's for Karine (and a physio, if needed).

## How to read it

Look at the direction over a month, not one weigh-in — weight moves about ±1 kg a day
from water and salt. If the scale is flat but clothes fit better, the plan is working.

## Changing the plan

Only when you ask. Just say what you'd like changed — a dish, a training day, portion
size — and the plan files get updated.
