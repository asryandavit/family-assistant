# Tracking

Progress numbers are **health records**, so they don't live in this repo (decision 079).
Each adult keeps their own private Google Sheet; this page says what to record, when,
and how the numbers change the plan.

## Where

| Who | Sheet | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Davit | Google Drive → Family Coach → **Progress — Davit** | Davit only, unless he shares it |
| Karine | Make a copy of Drive → Family Coach → **Progress — template (make a copy)** into Karine's own Drive (*File → Make a copy*) | Karine only, unless Karine shares it |
| Son | nothing tracked — growth is checked at the paediatrician | — |

The postpartum self-check (leaking, heaviness, pain, doming) is **never** written in a
Sheet or anywhere else — it's for Karine and, if needed, a physio (decision 067).

Later the Telegram bot will take `/weight`-style entries and keep them on the home PC;
at that point each Sheet's history is imported once and the Sheet becomes a mirror.

## What and when

| When | Davit | Karine |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 0 — before starting** | weight, waist, retests, **blood test (lipid panel: LDL, HDL, triglycerides, total)** | weight, waist (optional), retests |
| **Every Saturday morning** | weight, waist, strength/cardio sessions, average steps, stomach-symptom days, energy 1–5 | weight, waist, sessions, walks, energy 1–5 |
| **Weeks 4, 8, 12** | retests: push-ups max (write the level), sit-to-stand in 30 s, plank time | same |
| **About every 3 months** | repeat the blood test (doctor-ordered) | what Karine's doctor suggests |

**Weigh-in protocol:** Saturday, after the toilet, before eating, same scale, light
clothes. **Waist:** at navel level, end of a normal breath out, tape snug but not
compressing. Judge the **4-week trend**, never one reading — weight moves ±1 kg a day
from water and salt.

The baseline blood test matters: without a starting LDL there is no way to know what
the food changes did.

## How the numbers change the plan

Fixed rules, so nobody decides on a bad-scale morning (from `docs/COACH_SPEC.md` §6):

| Signal | Change |
|---|---|
| Weight trend flat for 4 weeks while following the plan ≥ 80% of the time, waist not falling | small change: slightly smaller grain portions or +1,000 steps/day — one change at a time |
| Weight flat but waist falling > 1 cm over 4 weeks | **no change** — that's fat loss with muscle kept, the plan is working |
| LDL not improved at the 3-month test | strengthen the cholesterol foods in the plan **and** discuss with the doctor |
| Exercise felt easy two sessions in a row | level up by [training/progression.md](training/progression.md) |
| Pain, symptoms, illness, travel | step back or pause — a pause is not a failure |

The Sunday 10-minute review ([habits.md](habits.md)) is where these get checked.
Medication is never part of this plan — that's the doctor's decision.
