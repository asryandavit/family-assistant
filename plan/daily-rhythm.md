# Daily rhythm

One shared timeline for the three of you, so nobody has to decide "when" every day.
Times are **anchors, not rules** — change them here and every plan follows.

**Assumptions to check and edit:** Davit works from home about 09:00–18:00; the son
naps once after lunch; his bedtime is 20:30. If Davit works in an office, move his
lunch walk and desk breaks to the office and keep everything else.

## Weekday

| Time | Davit | Karine | Son |
|---|---|---|---|
| 07:00 | Wake, glass of water | Wake, glass of water | Wake |
| 07:30 | **Breakfast** together (eggs / hercules / katnashor) | Breakfast | Breakfast |
| 08:00 | **Coffee #1** after breakfast · 5-min mobility (`mb-*`) | Coffee #1 | Free play |
| 09:00–13:00 | Work · **desk break every 30 min** (2–5 min, walk if possible) · 20-20-20 for eyes | Outdoor walk + playground with son (45–60 min) | **Active play outdoors** |
| 13:00 | **Lunch** — main meal (from batch) | Lunch | Lunch |
| 13:20 | **10–15 min walk** (stairs or outside) | — | Nap ≈ 13:30–15:30 |
| 13:45–15:30 | Work, desk breaks continue | **Own time while son naps:** training session (Mon/Wed/Fri, 15–25 min) or rest / a Scoolinary lesson | Nap |
| 15:30 | **Coffee #2** + small snack (fruit, matsun) — last coffee of the day | Coffee #2 + snack | Snack, play |
| 16:00–18:00 | Work, desk breaks; 1–2 "exercise snacks" (1–2 min brisk stairs) | Dinner prep from batch (15–20 min) · play | Play |
| 18:00 | Close the laptop → change clothes (habit cue) | — | — |
| 18:15 | **Training session** (see [family/davit.md](family/davit.md)) — 5 h after lunch | Join on family-session days | **Family movement time** 15 min: dance, animal walks, ball |
| 19:00 | **Dinner** — light | Dinner | Dinner |
| 19:30 | Short family walk or play (gentle — after dinner) | | Bath |
| 20:30 | — | — | **Bedtime** |
| 21:00 | 10 min: mobility + tomorrow's 2-minute check (what's for lunch, is anything to defrost/soak) | Same | Asleep |
| 22:30 | Wind-down breathing (`br-wind`), screens off | Same | |
| 23:00 | **Sleep** → 7–8 h | Sleep | (11–14 h incl. nap) |

Why these times:
- **Dinner at 19:00, bed at 23:00** keeps 3–4 h between the last meal and lying down
  (gastritis advice).
- **Training at 18:15** is ~5 h after lunch and 45 min before a light dinner, so hard
  effort never sits on a full stomach.
- **Last coffee by ~15:30–16:00** protects sleep — and sleep itself helps weight loss
  (people who sleep more eat noticeably less).
- **Desk breaks every 30 min** with a few minutes of walking had the best measured
  effect on blood sugar and blood pressure for people who sit all day.
